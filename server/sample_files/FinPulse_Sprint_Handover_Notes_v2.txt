### Section 1: Urgent Architectural Shifts & Contradictions
1. Database Migration:
During Sprint 8 security audit, the compliance officer raised strict concerns regarding transactional ACID guarantees for regulatory audits.
Consequently, we decided to migrate the primary database from MongoDB to PostgreSQL (v16).
All new core accounting ledger schemas were rewritten in PostgreSQL with foreign key constraints.
MongoDB is currently deprecated and should not be used for transactional persistence.

2. Cloud Deployment Shift:
Due to high data transfer and egress costs on AWS, leadership decided to migrate staging and production workloads to Google Cloud Platform (GCP Cloud Run).
Do not deploy to AWS ECS; all container targets are currently being prepared for GCP.
